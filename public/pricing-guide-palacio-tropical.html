The Property
Privacy, Power & Beachfront Splendour
Palacio Tropical is an extraordinary 10,500 sq ft beachfront estate set in the pristine, secluded coastline of Tambor, Costa Rica. Designed for complete privacy and uncompromising luxury, this villa is a preferred destination for dignitaries, public officials, and discerning travellers who demand the finest — without compromise. Seven en-suite bedrooms, each with private bath and air conditioning, accommodate up to 18 guests in sovereign comfort. A grand dining room seats 14, while multiple large gathering spaces flow seamlessly to an outside bar, al fresco dining terrace, and a large private swimming pool. The villa can be fully staffed with private chefs, housekeeping, and a dedicated concierge for excursions and outings. Located directly next to Palacio Musical, both properties can be combined to host up to 36 guests — perfect for grand celebrations, private events, and multi-family retreats.
