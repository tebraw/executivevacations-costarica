The Property
Authentic Costa Rica in the Hills of Atenas
Nestled in the lush hillside of Atenas — consistently ranked among the world's best climates — The Palms Villa Estate is a beautiful 4,700 sq ft mountain retreat that offers something the coastal villas cannot: an authentic, deeply personal Costa Rican experience in a tranquil, private setting. Four bedrooms (three upstairs with full air conditioning, one on the ground floor) accommodate up to eight guests in genuine comfort. Resort-style grounds with a private pool, tennis court, and verdant tropical gardens create an environment of total serenity. The property is lovingly maintained by a full-time resident caretaker, Don Manuel, ensuring a seamless and personal stay. Perfect for family vacations, intimate weddings, reunions, and corporate or personal retreats — The Palms Villa Estate offers the peace and culture of Costa Rica's heartland, just 32 miles from the Pacific coast and marina.
