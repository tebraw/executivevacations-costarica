The Property
The Ultimate Beachfront Experience
Experience the pinnacle of beachfront luxury in this spectacular 12,500 sq ft villa featuring three magnificent decks, each offering breathtaking Pacific Ocean views. Palacio Musical is a one-of-a-kind estate that blends sophisticated design with the raw natural beauty of Costa Rica's pristine Tambor coastline. The villa's seven en-suite bedrooms are crafted for the most discerning guests, accommodating up to 18 visitors in complete privacy and comfort. A professional music recording studio, whale watching observatory deck with Tiki Bar, private beach access, and enhanced security make this property truly without equal on the Pacific coast. Located directly next to Palacio Tropical, both villas can be combined to accommodate up to 36 guests — ideal for weddings, large family celebrations, corporate retreats, and private events of any scale.
