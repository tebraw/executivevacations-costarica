The Property
Where Pacific Views Meet Modern Luxury
Lean back and relax in this peaceful, stylish villa with magnificent Pacific Ocean views and a beautifully custom-designed swimming pool. Entirely newly constructed, The View House combines the finest contemporary finishes with the untouched natural beauty of Tambor's coastline — one of Costa Rica's most secluded and pristine stretches of Pacific shoreline. The villa offers four bedrooms, including one private en-suite, thoughtfully designed for comfort and privacy, accommodating up to eight guests with ease. Expansive outdoor terraces provide breathtaking panoramic ocean vistas, while the bespoke pool invites long, unhurried afternoons in the Costa Rican sun. The property also offers spectacular whale watching opportunities directly from the patio deck during migration season. Located just five minutes from Palacio Tropical and Palacio Musical, The View House is an ideal option for larger groups seeking additional accommodation, or for families who want the intimacy of a smaller villa without sacrificing proximity to the grandeur of our flagship estates.
