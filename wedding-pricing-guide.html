Special Period

Christmas & New Year Weddings

Getting married over the holidays? Our estates are among the most stunning places in Central America to celebrate. Due to peak demand and elevated operational costs, the Christmas and New Year period requires a dedicated custom quote.

Holiday Season · Dec 20 – Jan 4

Custom Pricing on Request

Christmas and New Year weddings are subject to special holiday rates for both villa rentals and catering. Pricing cannot be calculated from the standard packages above and must be confirmed directly with our team. A premium surcharge applies and availability is extremely limited.
